# Roadmap

Ideas for after v1, roughly in priority order. Want one sooner? Open an issue or
[sponsor the project on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/99proteam) (goal: $5,000/month).

## Next

- **AI-written scripts from a description**: `scriptreel draft "show how to invite a teammate" --url https://app.dev`
  explores the app and writes a `demo.yml` for you to review.
- **Ducking**: lower background music automatically under narration.
- **Text-to-speech voice-over**: turn `voiceover:` lines into an audio track, not just subtitles.

## Later

- **Multiple browser tabs and popups**: follow new tabs and OAuth windows in the same video.
- **Mobile device frames**: iPhone and Android frames with touch indicators instead of a cursor.
- Keyboard shortcut overlays (show `⌘K` when a shortcut is pressed).
- Highlight and spotlight effects (dim everything except one element).
- Intro and outro title cards with your logo.
- Faster rendering with parallel frame compositing.
- Firefox and WebKit support.

## Done

- v0.1.0: first release (see the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md)).
- Background music (`--music`) with looping and fades, and chapters from captions (embedded + YouTube timestamps).
